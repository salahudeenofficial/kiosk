## Luminosity Refinement
I have smoothed out the luminosity transition by introducing an intermediate "Inner Glow" layer.

- **Outer**: 15px width, 8px blur, opacity 0.6 (Soft background)
- **Middle**: 8px width, 4px blur, opacity 0.8 (Main body)
- **Inner**: 4px width, 1.5 blur, opacity 0.9 (The "bridge" between body and core)
- **Core**: 1.5px width, 0.4px blur, opacity 0.95 (Sharp definition, but not pixel-perfect hard)

This removes the harsh jump in brightness between the core and the glow, creating a much more natural, gas-like neon tube appearance where the light intensity falls off gradually from the center.
